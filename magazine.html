Commissioner’s Letter

Welcome back
to the cult.

Pilgrimage, Chop, Name Change Terror, and a first Sunday that already feels like a season.

Every August the same miracle: grown adults treat a draft table in Fredericksburg like the only pilgrimage that still matters. Hands go up. Boards fill. Someone needs an air pump. Someone else is already in a skirt and a wig. The year does not begin with Week 1. It begins with the weekend that makes Week 1 feel like an epilogue.

This issue of Lana Straw Weekly — house title, no corporate cousin — is the ledger of that opening act. Draft-weekend lore. The Boom Baum carousel: four Premier deals in roughly sixteen days, all from the league database. Dual-league scoreboards from ESPN live while house scoring tables catch their breath. NCT and Chop as Discord announced them. Pull quotes preserved exactly.

Week 1 is closed. The Kickers n Jigba put up 179.86. Otter sits atop Champeens. Boom asked for a fantasy therapist. Backdoor Heat found the floor. And the countdown remains the purest line in the server:

“364 days until the best weekend of the year.”
— The Wheaton Dick Whacker · #general · Aug 30
— The Straw editors

Auction Snapshot

Premier: 192 picks · 18 keepers · $2,410 · max $76

Champeens: 192 picks · 0 keepers · $2,376 · max $73

Source: Supabase draft aggregates

Before anyone touched a nominator list, The Jackal filed the cold open of the year from Dublin: a serial resort dooker, three elevators compromised, lobby compromised, staff armed with anti-poo powder, second night running. “I’m afraid that he is only getting more powerful.” Travel lore, elevated — or rather, not.

Closer to home, Amburgers requested an air pump for “the kicking balls in my luggage.” Jesus Neck Tattoo, ever the philosopher: “Not sure if I’m agreeing to a sexual act or genuine request for an inflation device.” The Wheaton Dick Whacker dialed the thermostat: more energy, or everyone’s running 40s — and he’d be drinking one either way.

Boom flew toward the weekend with a flat tire and a camera pointed at destiny. Outfit posts rolled in. Amburgers enjoyed a last night. OC002 asked the essential parental question: is my son going to the draft? The answer, as ever, was yes — and then some.

Inside the room: handwritten boards, laptops, pointing. Auction math from Supabase — Premier 192 picks, 18 keepers, $2,410 spent (max $76); Champeens 192 picks, zero keepers, $2,376 spent (max $73). Numbers from the database. Vibes from everywhere else.

On the Champeens side, induction was refreshingly honest. BTeamSpecialist: “Welcome to the cult .. errr league!” SJones: “Ngl- it does feel like I joined a cult.” Correct on both counts. Draft weekend is not a dress rehearsal for fantasy football. It is the sport.
